# Mod Guide — Key Systems

A quick reference for the most important mods in Coffees Aero SMP.

---

## Create: Aeronautics

The heart of the pack. Lets you assemble fully functional airships from Create contraptions.

**Getting started:**
1. Build a frame using any blocks
2. Add a **Propeller** (horizontal thrust) and **Lift Fan** (vertical)
3. Place an **Aeronautics Controller** — this is your helm
4. Right-click the controller to board and pilot

**Key components:**

| Part | Function |
|------|----------|
| Propeller | Forward/backward thrust |
| Lift Fan | Vertical ascent/descent |
| Rudder | Turning control |
| Ballast Tank | Fine altitude control |
| Aeronautics Controller | Pilot interface |

---

## Create: TFMG (The Factory Must Grow)

Adds heavy industry to the Create ecosystem.

- **Oil Pump** — extract crude oil from underground deposits
- **Refinery** — process crude into diesel, lubricant, and naphtha
- **Steel Production** — blast furnace → steel ingots for structural builds
- Diesel powers **Diesel Engines**, a high-output power source

---

## Create: Steam 'n' Rails

Full train and railway system on top of Create.

- Craft **Train Tracks** and **Train Stations**
- Assemble **Locomotive** + carriages as a contraption
- Schedule automated cargo routes via **Schedule Boards**
- Pairs great with Aeronautics for a land ↔ air logistics network

---

## Create: Numismatics

Player economy mod designed for SMP.

- **Coins** can be minted from metals
- **Trading Depots** allow automated player-to-player shops
- **Pockets** are wearable wallets with no drop-on-death by default

---

## Sable Protect

Lets you lock your airship so other players can't steal or grief it.

- Crouch + right-click the **Aeronautics Controller** to open the protection menu
- Add trusted players who can pilot your ship
- Server admins can override locks

---

## Xaero's Minimap & World Map

- Press **`Y`** to open minimap settings
- Press **`M`** to open the full world map
- Right-click on the world map to set **waypoints** — useful for marking your airship hangar, base, and trading posts

---

## Waystones

- Craft a **Waystone** and place it at key locations
- Right-click to name it and teleport to known waystones
- Admins can configure free-teleport or XP cost per warp

---

## Simple Voice Chat

- Automatic proximity voice — no setup needed beyond installing the mod
- Hold **`V`** to talk
- Press **`G`** to open voice chat settings and group channels
- Range is set to **48 blocks** — ideal for close crew communication on airships

---

## Terralith

Overhauls the overworld with 100+ new biomes. Highlights visible from the air:
- **Yellowstone** — dramatic geothermal fields
- **Glacial Chasm** — deep ice canyon
- **Skylands** — floating island biomes (land your airship here)
- **Ancient Sands** — vast desert with ruins

No special setup needed — Terralith works as a datapack-style mod and integrates automatically with world generation.
