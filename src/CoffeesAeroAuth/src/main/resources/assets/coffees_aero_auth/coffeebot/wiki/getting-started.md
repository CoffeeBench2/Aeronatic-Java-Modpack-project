# Getting Started

## 1. Install the pack

The current pack is **Season 3 — version 3.0.2**. There are two ways in.

### Modrinth (recommended)

Install **Coffee's Create: Aeronautics SMP** through the Modrinth App, Prism
Launcher, ATLauncher, or any launcher that accepts an `.mrpack`.

> [!important] Launch twice the first time
> A few mods (FTB and the mods that depend on it) can't be hosted on Modrinth, so the
> pack downloads them itself on the **first launch**. Start the game, let it finish at
> the title screen, **close it, and start it again**. After that it behaves normally.

### CurseForge

The Season 3 pack for the CurseForge app is waiting on CurseForge's review. Until it's
listed, **grab the CurseForge pack from our [Discord](https://discord.gg/AnFUh5vTz6)**
and import it: CurseForge app → *Create Custom Profile* → *Import* → choose the zip.
It's self-contained and keeps itself up to date like the Modrinth version.

## 2. Give it the right amount of RAM

**8 GB is the sweet spot.** Season 3 handles more if your machine has room.

| Allocation | Result |
|---|---|
| Under 6 GB | Runs out of memory, stutters badly |
| **8 GB** | **What most people should use** |
| 10–12 GB | Fine **if** you have 32 GB or more in your PC — helps with Distant Horizons and big airship builds |
| More than half your PC's RAM | Asking for trouble — your graphics driver and Distant Horizons need memory **outside** Minecraft's share |

If your game vanishes while loading with no crash report, you've given it more than
your machine can spare. Come back down. More on this in **[[performance]]**.

## 3. Join

Press **Join** on the pack's main menu, or add the server:

```
coffeeaerosmp.duckdns.org
```

No port needed.

## 4. First login

You'll arrive in the **lobby** before you reach the world.

- **Offline (non-premium) players** register a password on first join with
  `/register <password> <password>` (the same password twice) and use
  `/login <password>` afterwards. You have **3 minutes** to log in before you're
  kicked — just reconnect if that happens.
- **Premium players** are verified automatically.

From the lobby you're sent on to the survival world.

## 5. Open your mail

Your **Season 3 starter kit** — **300 spurs**, bread and saplings — is waiting in
your mailbox. Type **`/mail`** and claim it. Level-up rewards, `/daily` and vote
rewards all arrive there too, and you'll hear a ping when something new lands.

## 6. Before you build anything

Read **[[claims]]**. Unclaimed builds are not protected, you start with **5 chunks**
(more as you level), and there's one rule about ships that will cost you your claim
if you get it wrong.

## Updating

The pack updates itself — you'll be prompted from the main menu, and the game closes
to apply the update. Let it finish and relaunch.
