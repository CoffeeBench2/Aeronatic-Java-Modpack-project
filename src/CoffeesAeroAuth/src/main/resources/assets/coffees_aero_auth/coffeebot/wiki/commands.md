# Commands

Cooldowns below are the live server settings.

## Getting around

Season 3 keeps teleporting to a minimum — the world is for flying, riding and building
across. These are the ways to jump:

| Command | What it does | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| `/spawn` | Back to world spawn | 1 minute |
| `/home` | To your bed / respawn point | 5 minutes |
| `/rtp` | Random teleport into the wilderness (Overworld only) | 15 minutes |
| `/back` | Back to where you died or last teleported from (within 5 minutes) | 3 minutes |

`/tpa` and warps are **switched off** for Season 3 — you can't teleport to other
players. Set your bed somewhere sensible, and build a railway or an airship.

### About `/back`

After a **5-second countdown** you arrive as a **ghost** for up to 60 seconds: nothing
can hurt you (players included) and mobs ignore you, so you can get your things back
from your corpse safely. The ghost ends early as soon as you interact with anything
other than your corpse.

None of these work while you're **combat-tagged** — see below.

### About `/rtp`

It drops you between **1,500 and 10,000 blocks** out — far enough to be somewhere
nobody has been, close enough to get home.

**Stand perfectly still** from the moment you type it until you arrive. The server is
preparing your destination and needs you parked while it does; moving cancels it. If
someone else is already teleporting you'll be queued, with your position shown — still
stand still. A cancelled or timed-out teleport **doesn't** use up your cooldown.

## Ships

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/shipname <name>` | Name the ship you're standing on |
| `/shipname info` | Show the current name |
| `/shipname clear` | Remove the name |

See [[airships]] and [[claims]].

## Account

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/register <password> <password>` | Set your password on first join, typed twice (offline players) |
| `/login <password>` | Log in (offline players) |
| `/changepassword <old> <new>` | Change your password |
| `/skin <name>` | Set your skin — **offline players can use this too** |

You have **3 minutes** to log in before being kicked. Just reconnect if it happens.

## Help from staff

| Command | What it does | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| `/ticket <message>` | Send staff a ticket with your location. Replies arrive by mail | 2 a day |
| `/ticket list` | Your tickets and their status | — |

## Discord

Run these in the server's Discord, not in-game:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/uptime` | How long the SMP has been running |
| `/leaderboard` | Top 10 by playtime |
| `/link <code>` | Link your Discord to your in-game account (get the code with `/discord link` in game) |

You can also **mention CoffeeBot** with a question — "how do I claim land?", "is the
server up?", "what's my level?" — and it answers straight away.

## Things worth knowing

- **The Nether opens 24 hours after launch** (Wednesday 8 October, 18:30 IST). Until
  then portals won't take you there
- **The End is closed**
- **PvP is on**
- **Combat logging is punished** — a hit tags you for **30 seconds**. Disconnect while
  tagged and you die, with your items dropping where the fight was
- **Idle for 10 minutes** and you're sent to the lobby — see [[progression]]
- **Dropped items are cleared every 30 minutes**, with warnings in chat before it
  happens. Named items are kept
