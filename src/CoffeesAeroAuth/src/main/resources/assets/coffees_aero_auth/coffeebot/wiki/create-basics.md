# New to Create?

You don't need to know Create to join. This is the shortest path from "just
spawned" to "flying my own airship" — skip anything you already know.

---

## The one idea Create is built on

Create runs on **rotational force**. Something spins, that spin travels along
shafts and cogwheels, and machines consume it to do work.

Two numbers matter:

- **Speed (RPM)** — how fast it turns. Some machines need a minimum
- **Stress** — how much work you're asking for. Generators supply it, machines
  consume it. Exceed the supply and everything stops

When your contraption dies for no obvious reason, it's almost always stress. Add
another generator.

## Your first machine

1. **Andesite Alloy** is the gateway item — zinc and iron. Make a lot; everything
   needs it
2. **Cogwheels and shafts** move rotation around. Large and small cogs together
   change the speed
3. A **Water Wheel** on flowing water is the easiest first generator. Cheap,
   quiet, no fuel
4. Feed that into a **Millstone** or **Mechanical Press** and you've automated
   your first thing

Point a **Goggles**-equipped face at any Create block to see its speed and stress
readout. Wear them constantly while learning — Create tells you exactly what's
wrong if you look.

## Moving up

- **Windmills** scale better than water wheels once you have room
- **Steam** is the real answer later — more setup, far more output
- **Mechanical Belts** for items, **Fluid Pipes** for liquids
- **Trains** once you want to connect bases

## Then: airships

Once you're comfortable assembling contraptions, **Create: Aeronautics** is the
step up — real flying ships you build and pilot.

Go to [[airships]] when you're ready. Build small first; a tiny test ship teaches
you the controls without risking hours of work.

## Things that trip up new players here

**Claim before you build.** See [[claims]]. Unclaimed builds aren't protected, you
start with only 5 chunks, and land claims are full-height columns — so someone's
claim above you affects you too.

**Stress before speed.** Almost every "it stopped working" is insufficient stress
capacity, not a broken build.

**Give the game about 8 GB of RAM** — more only if your PC has room to spare. See [[performance]].

**Ask.** The [Discord](https://discord.gg/AnFUh5vTz6) is full of people who have
already made the mistake you're about to make.
