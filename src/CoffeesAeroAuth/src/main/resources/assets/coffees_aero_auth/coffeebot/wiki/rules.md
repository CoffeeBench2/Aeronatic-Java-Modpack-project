# Rules

Short version: **don't take other people's stuff, don't cheat, don't be a
nuisance.** The rest is detail.

Some of these are enforced automatically by the server. Where that's the case
it's marked — those aren't judgement calls, the server just does it.

---

## 1. Don't take or break what isn't yours

Griefing, stealing and raiding other people's builds get you removed.

**Claiming is how you protect things** — see [[claims]]. An unclaimed build is
not protected by the server, but "it wasn't claimed" is **not** a defence for
taking it. The rule applies regardless.

## 2. No cheating

X-ray, cheat clients, fly hacks, duplication glitches, macro-farming while you're
away from the keyboard. Any of these and you're gone.

Client-side mods that only change how the game *looks* are fine — minimaps,
shaders, sound mods. If you're unsure, ask before you install it.

**Found a dupe or an exploit?** Report it in Discord instead of using it. We'd
much rather fix it, and people who report get credit rather than a ban.

## 3. PvP and combat

PvP is **on**. If you don't want a fight, don't start one and don't linger where
one is happening.

> [!warning] Combat logging is enforced by the server
> Disconnect while combat-tagged and you **die**, dropping your items where the
> fight was. This isn't a punishment we apply by hand — it's automatic.

Spawn-camping and repeatedly hunting the same new player is harassment, and gets
treated as such.

## 4. Ships and claims

- **Place → claim → name.** Capturing a claimed ship with a portable structure
  item orphans its claim and leaves an abandoned ship behind. See [[airships]]
- Don't park ships on top of someone else's claimed land without asking
- Don't build in a way designed to block someone else's access

## 5. Chat and community

- English in main chat so staff can moderate it
- No slurs, harassment, or sexual content
- No advertising other servers
- Chat is bridged to Discord — the same standards apply in both directions

## 6. Alt accounts

One account per person for claim purposes. Using alts to multiply your chunk
allowance isn't allowed.

Sharing an account is at your own risk — whatever happens on it is your
responsibility.

## 7. AFK

Idle for **10 minutes** and the server sends you back to the lobby, and that time
doesn't count toward your playtime or level. AFK machines and auto-clickers to dodge
it count as cheating under rule 2.

## 8. Lag and server health

Enormous item-drop piles, runaway mob farms and giant always-on contraptions
affect everyone. If staff ask you to shut something down or scale it back, do it.

We'd rather help you redesign it than delete it, so talk to us early.

---

## What happens if you break a rule

Most things start with a warning, because most things are misunderstandings.
Griefing, cheating and harassment skip straight to a removal.

Think a decision was wrong? Appeal in
**[Discord](https://discord.gg/AnFUh5vTz6)**. Argue it there, not in chat.

---

## Staff will never ask for your password

Nobody on staff needs it, ever. Anyone asking is not staff.
