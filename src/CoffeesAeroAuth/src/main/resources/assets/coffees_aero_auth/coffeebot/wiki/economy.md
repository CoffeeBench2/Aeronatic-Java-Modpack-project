# Economy, Mail & Rewards

The currency is **spurs**, from Create: Numismatics — so money is a physical item you
can put on a belt, not a number in a menu.

---

## Your mailbox

```
/mail
```

**Everything the server gives you arrives as mail** — your starter kit, level
rewards, `/daily`, votes, and gifts from staff. Nothing drops into your inventory by
itself, so a full inventory never eats a reward.

- When new mail lands while you're online you get a chat message and a **ping**
- When you log in, you're told whether you have unread mail
- Claim the attachments from the mailbox screen. Unclaimed mail expires after
  **60 days**, so don't leave it forever

You can send mail to other players too. Coins and other Numismatics items can't be
mailed — money changes hands in the world.

## Spurs

Spurs are coins. They stack, they drop when you die, and they can be stored,
transported and automated like any other item. Numismatics adds vaults and the
machines that let you build actual shops.

The server only ever pays spurs in two ways: your **starter kit** and **voting**.
Everything else is the player economy. That's deliberate — it keeps coins worth something.

Because coins are items, **don't carry your savings into a fight.**

## Starter kit

Your **Season 3 welcome mail** holds **300 spurs, 16 bread and 4 oak saplings**.
One per player, waiting in `/mail` from your first visit.

## Daily reward

```
/daily
```

Claimable every **20 hours**, in the Overworld. The reward follows a **7-day cycle**
of items plus XP. The XP lands immediately; the items arrive in your mailbox.

There's a streak. Leave it longer than **40 hours** (double the interval) and it
resets, so claiming at roughly the same time each day keeps it alive.

## Voting

Run `/vote` for the link. Voting pays **spurs and diamonds**, and the reward grows
the more days in a row you vote:

| | Day 1 | Each day after | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spurs | 2 | +1 | **10** |
| Diamonds | 1 | +1 | **5** |

- You can vote once every **24 hours**. Miss more than **48 hours** and the streak
  starts again
- **Your first 30 votes are rewarded.** After that voting still helps the server, it
  just stops paying
- **Rewards arrive by mail the moment you vote**, even if you're offline — vote from
  your phone and it's waiting when you log in

## Where the rest of your money comes from

- **Selling to other players.** Numismatics exists to make player shops work. The
  Discord is where most trade gets arranged
- **Automation.** This is a Create server. The intended answer to "how do I get more
  of something" is usually a machine
